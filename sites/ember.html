01 The fire

One grill.Lit at two.

Every afternoon we lay the fire with binchotan and split oak from a Sussex coppice. By five it has burned down to a white, even heat, the lamb shoulder has had its six hours and the flatbread dough has proved twice. There is no gas in our kitchen — if it’s hot, it came off the coals.
