[05] FacilitiesWhat's in the arch

The arch

210 m² under the Victorian viaduct: rubber floor, high brick vault, trains rumbling overhead every few minutes. You stop noticing by week two. Everything is within arm's reach and nothing is just for show.

  1. 01Platforms

    Four 3 m lifting platforms, calibrated bumper plates, competition bars

  2. 02Kettlebells

    Competition bells 8–48 kg, matched pairs up to 32 kg

  3. 03Engine room

    Three air bikes, two rowers and a ski erg

  4. 04Sled lane

    20 m turf track running the length of the arch

  5. 05Rig

    Six-station pull-up rig, rings and landmines

  6. 06Recovery

    Mobility corner with rollers, bands and a stretch area

  7. 07Changing

    Two showers, lockers and towels included

  8. 08Extras

    Chalk station, filtered water, bike storage inside
