(01) The idea

A bright little room on Mare Street for people who take their coffee slowly — and their mornings quickly.

Baked at 6 a.m.

Cardamom buns, brown butter cookies and banana bread, out of the oven before the shutters go up.

A guest roaster, every month

A different roaster's beans each month, pulled as espresso and poured as batch brew, with free refills before 9 a.m.

Ready in about four minutes

Order on your phone, walk in, and grab it from the shelf by the door. No queue before your train.
